We work best as a team

MiVi Studio is a multidisciplinary architecture studio from Belgrade. We work in architecture, interior design, 3D modeling, visualizations and animations, and we explore architecture through drawing and analog photography.

We met in our first semester at the Faculty of Architecture in Belgrade and started working together immediately, first on university projects, then on our first commissions. We completed our master's degrees in 2021, and our own studio was a natural continuation.

Every project begins with understanding the task and the people we're doing it for. Between the first idea and the finished space are many steps, conversations, agreements and adjustments. We take that journey together with our clients and the craftspeople we build with.

We believe architecture can positively affect people and the environment. That's why we don't chase trends: we look for harmonious architecture, the kind that doesn't go out of style.
