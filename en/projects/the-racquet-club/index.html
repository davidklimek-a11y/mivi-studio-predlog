The Racquet Club

A club where people stay long after the last point.

MiVi Studio
The Racquet Club
Type
Sports club and restaurant
Location
Belgrade, Partizan Tennis Club
Year
2024–2026
Area
hall approx. 1,350 m², ground floor 450 m²
Scope
Interiors of the sports hall, restaurant, reception, changing rooms and Kids Club; a refreshed tennis stadium
Photography
Ljubo Ašćerić

The task included a new hall for padel and pickleball of approx. 1,350 m², refurbishment of 450 m² of the ground floor of the existing building for the club's ancillary facilities, and a refreshed look for the tennis stadium. The building was in the shell-and-core stage, and the hall did not exist, so we could organize the space from scratch for its new purpose.

The ground floor houses the restaurant, reception, changing rooms, kitchen and Kids Club, a space for the youngest members. The clients wanted a design that draws on the history of tennis in a contemporary interpretation, with the maximum possible number of padel courts, without giving up comfort or clear sightlines.

The atmosphere is borrowed from tennis tournaments and old clubs: green hues, the color of clay, wood, textiles, retro details, trophies and graphics. The restaurant is dominated by warm wood combined with porcelain stoneware.

Many elements were designed and made to measure specifically for this space: benches around the courts, a chandelier made of rackets in the entrance area, wall prints, the reception desk's arched counter, and the bar and shelves in the restaurant.
