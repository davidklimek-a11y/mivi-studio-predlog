Villa with an inner courtyard

Conceptual design · 2023

A luxurious villa that celebrates its natural setting. Of its many distinct parts, the inner courtyard stands out: greenery and water create a serene oasis apart from the city.

Model: MiVi Studio · Render: xzzi.studio

Residential building in Novi Sad

Conceptual design · 2022

A mezzanine of two ellipsoid volumes and a shared terrace separates homes from business space and can serve as an exhibition area or offices. Green terraces on the corners soften the building's relationship with its monotonous context, and the facade is turned slightly west for more natural light.

Visualizations: xzzi.studio

An old hospital, new homes

Conversion into a residential complex · 2022

The reconstruction of an old hospital into a residential complex, in partnership with the architect Nebojša Glišić. The project preserves and revitalizes the building's authentic state while subtly integrating contemporary design.
