Tyman na vodi

One line of light leads through the entire space.

MiVi Studio
Tyman na vodi
Type
Cocktail and shisha bar
Location
Belgrade
Year
2025
Area
approx. 200 m²
Scope
Bar interior, built in three months
Photography
Relja Ivanić

“Through the design we wanted to honor the function of a shisha and cocktail bar, while creating an atmosphere with a touch of mystery and a pronounced night-time character, which at the same time remains relaxed and pleasant for guests to stay longer.”

The main motif is a fluid light tube that follows the line of the interior and connects all zones into a whole. The bar counter is the focal point: around it branch seating areas, from more open and lively to more intimate ones for longer stays. We used the existing concrete columns for natural zoning.

Burgundy and green tones break up the neutral base and accentuate individual areas. The team was large, and the entire project was built in three months, with good coordination of all teams.
