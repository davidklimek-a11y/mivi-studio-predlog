MiVi Studio
Duplex in Belgrade
Type
Apartment interior
Location
Belgrade
Year
2025
Scope
Interior of a two-level apartment with made-to-measure elements
Photography
Relja Ivanić

Right from the entrance, the apartment feels open and connected. The kitchen, dining area and living room flow into one another, with each area clearly readable, without conventional partitions.

The double height, large glazed surfaces and vertical accents enhance the sense of space. A suspended structure with a bio-fireplace and a glazed study on the gallery introduce dynamism and serve as landmarks. We didn't hide the load-bearing column at the entrance to the kitchen: it became part of the composition.

“Special emphasis was placed on natural materials, a calm color palette with accents, and a clear hierarchy of zones.” Textures, fine details and the play of light and shadow bring the neutral palette to life.

The night zone on the upper floor is completely private: calming tones, wardrobes and the bathroom create a warm oasis for rest after a long day.
